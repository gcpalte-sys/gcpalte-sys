## Hi there 👋, I'm Grant

I'm an undergraduate Physics student at The Ohio State University pursuing a minor in Computer and Information Science (CIS). My primary focus is on quantum computing, experimental condensed matter physics, and the intersection of hardware and quantum information science (QIS).

### 🔭 What I'm working on

- **Quantum Computing:** Exploring hardware modalities and practical applications in QIS, with hands-on experience using **PennyLane** and **Qiskit**.
- **Experimental Physics:** Working as a Research Assistant under Dr. Jay Gupta on ultra-high vacuum (UHV) surface science: gas intercalation in graphite (HOPG), UHV chamber work, and the instrument-control and analysis software that runs our experiments.
- **Academic Leadership:** Serving as an officer/executive board member for the Ohio State Quantum Computing Club (co-organizing **Qiskit Fall Fest 2026**) and as a Research Coordinator for the Undergraduate Research Society.

### 🛠️ Featured projects

- **[pbn-heater-ramp-controller](https://github.com/gcpalte-sys/pbn-heater-ramp-controller):** Closed-loop temperature controller for a UHV heating stage, with physics-based feedforward, gain-scheduled PI, and layered hardware safety.
- **[cqnn-stock-prediction](https://github.com/gcpalte-sys/cqnn-stock-prediction):** PennyLane replication of a contextual quantum neural network for multi-stock prediction.
- **[microscope-tile-stitcher](https://github.com/gcpalte-sys/microscope-tile-stitcher):** Stitches optical microscope tile scans of HOPG samples into seamless mosaics using SIFT matching and a global least-squares solve.
